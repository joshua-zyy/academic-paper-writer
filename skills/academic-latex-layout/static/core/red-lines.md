# Red Lines

Do not cross these lines without explicit user approval and a written risk note.

- Do not globally convert floats to `[H]` or load `float` only to force every object in place.
- Do not use repeated negative `\vspace`, `\vskip`, or spacing hacks to hide page-limit or float problems.
- Do not insert blind `\clearpage` or `\newpage` commands just to flush deferred floats.
- Do not move a figure/table away from the paragraph that explains it merely to balance a page.
- Do not delete, shorten, or rewrite scientific claims to make layout fit without confirmation.
- Do not split, merge, crop, or downscale figures/tables in a way that changes meaning or harms readability without confirmation.
- Do not edit official `.cls`, `.sty`, or venue template files unless the user explicitly asks.
- Do not add packages that may conflict with the venue template without checking compatibility and asking when risk is nontrivial.
- Do not claim the layout is fixed if compilation or PDF preview was feasible but skipped.
- Do not invent missing assets, captions, labels, claims, or venue rules.
